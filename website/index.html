Meet Molt

A hermit crab that trades its heavy shell for a lighter one. Your Mac can do the same.

Private by default

No account, no analytics, no network access.

Open source

Every rule is readable Swift code, licensed under GPL-3.0.

See what fills your disk.

The overview splits your Mac's storage by category, the way System Settings does. Each category opens a list you can sort, filter and clean.
