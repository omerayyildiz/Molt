A cleaner you can trust

Explains every item

What it is, how safe it is to remove and how it comes back. Nothing is checked until you check it.

Only rebuildable files

A fixed list of rules decides what Molt may touch. Documents, photos, settings, mail and iCloud are never on it.

Safe by design

Every item is checked again right before removal. Files that do not come back go to the Trash. No administrator password, ever.

Private and open

No account, no analytics, no network. Every rule is readable Swift code on GitHub under the GPL.

See what it is before it goes

Each category lists its items with a trust level, when they last changed and how much space they take. Filter by trust or age to find projects you have not opened in months.

  • SafeComes back by itself or with the next build.
  • CautionComes back by downloading or installing again.
  • RiskyDoes not come back, so it goes to the Trash.
