The first release of Molt, a free and open source Mac app that clears the files your developer tools leave behind.

**Download Molt.dmg below, open it and drag Molt to Applications.** Requires macOS 15 or later.

### What Molt finds

- **Xcode:** DerivedData, SwiftUI previews, device support and archives
- **Project dependencies:** node_modules, .venv, Pods, target, .next, .build and Gradle build folders
- **Package caches:** npm, npx, pnpm, Yarn, Bun, Homebrew, pip, uv, Cargo, Gradle and CocoaPods
- **AI models:** Ollama, LM Studio and Hugging Face
- **Simulators, iOS installers, device backups, app caches and logs**

### How it keeps you safe

- Every item says what it is, how safe it is to remove and how it comes back.
- Nothing is selected until you select it.
- Files that can't be rebuilt, such as Xcode archives and device backups, go to the Trash.
- Molt never touches Documents, Photos, Mail or iCloud, never asks for an administrator password and makes no
  network requests.

### Also in 1.0
